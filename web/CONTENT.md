# Power Lab content guide

The earlier 30 levels remain intact. The new six-level chapter introduces delivery and routing mechanics rather than extending the old hazard/survival layouts.

| Level | Puzzle | Required new interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 31 | Special Delivery | Route safe energy cargo into its receiving dock; survival alone does not win. |
| 32 | Air Freight | Use a force field to carry cargo across a gap, then stop its horizontal motion over the dock. Removing the fan breaks the reference solution. |
| 33 | Cut the Beam | Occlude an actual laser ray with ink before its warmup completes. The nearest polygon surface blocks the beam. |
| 34 | Shield and Ship | Use one continuous stroke to deliver cargo and protect a robot from a laser until the survival timer ends. Early delivery alone does not win. |
| 35 | Color Dispatch | Sort two separately marked cores into their matching docks at different heights. A wrong-color delivery fails. |
| 36 | Stamp and Send | Use an updraft and ramp to route a core through its inspection ring before the upper receiving dock. Stamps belong to individual cores. |

Cyan cargo/docks carry a plus symbol; amber cargo/docks carry a triangle. Rings show an arrow before inspection and a check after contact. This makes the new mechanics legible without relying on color alone. Energy cores are not lethal hazards.

The chapter earns a Power Lab Hero badge at level 36. Saved progress and earned paints from prior chapters persist.

Validation includes unique mechanic fingerprints and no exact layout duplicates, real Matter.js solutions and ineffective-stroke failures for all 36 levels, small stroke variations, targeted browser playthrough of levels 31–36, and mobile touch/save migration. The new layouts have not been human playtested or certified on physical devices.

# Grand Circuit content guide

| Level | Puzzle | Required interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 41 | Opposing Currents | Catch two colors driven into opposite cargo lanes. Both backstops are needed. |
| 42 | Triple Clearance | Complete three ordered inspections while shielding Pip from a beam. |
| 43 | Shutter Bridge | Build a bridge and an overhead beam canopy with a continuous route around the charger. |
| 44 | Parallel Airlocks | Route separately marked cores through independent upward lanes and their own inspection rings. |
| 45 | Windwalk | Use a robot-only lift across a missing floor and shield the crossing from above. |
| 46 | Ceiling Elevator | Keep a rising robot below a shielded ceiling to reach the elevated charging deck. |
| 47 | Receiving Stack | Fill two receiving bays; the upper delivery opens the exit gate. |
| 48 | Split Inspection | Inspect both colors, catch them at different heights, and protect Pip from a beam. |
| 49 | Voltage Vault | Route a core while shielding Pip against three beam directions. |
| 50 | Grand Circuit | Give both parcels three ordered stamps, fill both upper docks, and rescue both robots across the bridge. |

The campaign ends at 50 levels and awards a Grand Circuit Hero badge. All ten new puzzles have authored winning strokes, small sampling-variation checks, ineffective-stroke failures, and a real browser playthrough. The final layout includes drawing-boundary clearance for mouse/touch quantization.
