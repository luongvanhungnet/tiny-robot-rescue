Privacy Policy — Tiny Robot Rescue

Effective Date: 2026-11-13

Tiny Robot Rescue is an offline-capable 2D physics puzzle game. Your privacy is important to us.

1. Offline Gameplay & Local Storage

All level progress, star ratings, unlocked robot skins, settings, and local gameplay statistics are stored strictly on your device. No account registration is required. Note that uninstalling the app or clearing app data may permanently delete local progress.

2. Optional Advertising & Consent

You can choose to play without personalized advertising. Where optional rewarded or interstitial ads are enabled in production releases, third-party ad SDKs (such as Google AdMob) may process device identifiers and coarse diagnostic data subject to your consent choice and applicable regional laws.

3. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases (such as removing between-level interstitial ads) are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

4. Children's Privacy

Tiny Robot Rescue does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any ad configuration adheres to Google Play Families and target-audience declarations configured in Google Play Console.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or support, please open an issue or contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing support email.
